How it works

Built so buyers actually trust what they are getting

Verified seller profiles

Every stall owner is identity checked and onboarded in person before they're listed

Condition ratings

Items are rated A-C on condition by the seller and confirmed by buyer reviews after pickup

Escrow-lite payments

Your money is held by Thriftly and only released to the seller once you confirm the item matches.

How a payment actually moves

1
Buyer pays

Funds sent to Thriftly, not directly to the seller

2
Funds held

Thriftly holds payment until pickup or delivery

3
Buyer confirms

Item checked against the listed condition rating

4
Seller paid

Funds released minus Thriftly's commission

Why we built this

Existing alternatives weren't working

Right now, finding a specific stall means paid brokers, freinds referrals, or searching market alleys yourself. We replace that with a verified, searchable platform.

The problem

  • Time consuming to find specific stalls
  • Price hiking based on how a buyer looks
  • Hard to access stalls at the back of markets

Our solution

  • Verified seller profiles, no guesswork
  • Searchable stall listings by category and market
  • Escrow lite paymets and condition ratings
