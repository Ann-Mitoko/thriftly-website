 App Summary

Thriftly is a community-focused web platform that connects buyers  and resellers with verified thrift stall owners across Nairobi's markets. It targets young people aged 15–30 and budget-conscious shoppers who struggle to find affordable quality clothing. The platform solves the problem of time-consuming market searches, price-hiking by brokers, and hard-to-find stalls by providing verified seller profiles, searchable listings by category and location, condition ratings, and an escrow-lite payment flow.


Resources our app stores:
1. Vendors — thrift stall owners and their listing details
2. Categories — types of clothing sold (Vintage, Streetwear,
   Kids, Accessories, Bulk)
3. Markets — physical market locations in Nairobi
4. Reviews — buyer ratings and comments on vendor profiles
5. Users — buyers and resellers registered on the platform

Actions users can take:
- View all verified vendors
- View a single vendor's full details
- Filter vendors by category
- Search vendors by name, area or description
- Submit a new vendor listing via the List Your Stall form
- Leave a review and rating on a vendor
- Register as a buyer or reseller
- Switch between buyer and reseller browsing mode
- Admin can approve or reject pending vendor submissions
